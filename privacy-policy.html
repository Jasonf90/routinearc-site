Privacy Policy

RoutineArc is offline-first and privacy-first. This page describes everything the app does — and deliberately does not do — with your information.

Effective 2026-08-31 · Applies to the RoutineArc app for iPhone

OverviewUser guide

Your data stays on your device

RoutineArc stores every task, event, and note in an on-device SQLite database. There is no account and no cloud sync, and nothing you write is uploaded to us — we run no server to upload it to.

Your content leaves the device only in the specific cases described below, and each one is either optional or something you start yourself: optional address lookup, opening a map, exporting a backup, and buying or restoring an unlock through the App Store.

Microphone

Microphone access is used solely to convert your speech into tasks, events, and notes while you are using voice capture. RoutineArc asks for on-device recognition through iOS's own speech framework, so your audio is not sent to us and normally does not leave your phone. If your device cannot recognise your language on-device, RoutineArc offers you a choice: decline and type it instead, or accept and let Apple transcribe that recording on its servers under Apple's privacy policy. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you accept, and the choice is per session, never remembered silently.

Camera & text scanning

If you scan an image, the camera/photo access is used only to capture that image, and text recognition (OCR) runs entirely on-device via Apple's Vision framework. Images and recognized text are never uploaded or stored beyond the task/event you create.

Location

If you grant location access, RoutineArc uses it only to estimate drive time to tasks and events that have a location. That estimate is computed entirely on your device — no routing or traffic service is contacted, and your coordinates are never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

Looking up an address is a separate, optional feature. Apple's geocoder is network-backed, so enabling it sends the address you typed — or, for "use my current location", your coordinates — to the system geocoding service and OpenStreetMap Nominatim. It is OFF by default, and you can turn it on in Settings. Saved places store their coordinates once, so drive time keeps working with it off. Location is optional throughout.

On-device AI

AI features run entirely on your iPhone. Understanding what you type or say, summarising a note and suggesting subtasks use Apple Intelligence, which is built into iOS and runs on the device's Neural Engine; day planning and priority are ordinary arithmetic over your schedule rather than a model at all. There is no model to download and nothing to sign in to, and your content is never sent to a server for processing. If Apple Intelligence is unavailable, a deterministic rules engine takes over and also runs entirely on-device.

No third-party analytics

RoutineArc adds no analytics, tracking, or crash reporting, and embeds no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. The system frameworks it uses — Vision for text recognition, Core Location for location, and Apple Intelligence for AI — are part of iOS and are governed by Apple’s own privacy policy. We do not receive any data from them.

Buying and restoring

RoutineArc is free for seven days, after which capturing new items needs a one-time unlock or a yearly subscription. Purchases go through the App Store, so your payment details are handled entirely by Apple and are never seen by this app.

What RoutineArc learns is one thing: whether the Apple Account on this device holds an unlock. Not your name, not your Apple Account address, not a payment method, and not what else you have bought. That check runs on the device itself — there is still no RoutineArc account and no server of ours involved.

The seven days are counted from the date the App Store records for your first download of the app, which is read on the device and not stored anywhere else.

Notifications

Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No notification content leaves your phone.

Export is in your hands

The only way to take your data OUT in bulk is Export data in Settings, which hands a JSON copy to the app you choose via the iOS share sheet.

Data collection

RoutineArc collects no personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising identifier, and no tracking across apps or websites. We operate no server that receives your content.

Children

RoutineArc is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
